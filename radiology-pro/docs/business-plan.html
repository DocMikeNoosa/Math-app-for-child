Size of the market

  • About 3,900–4,300 radiology specialists; under 3,000 actively practising. About 19% are over 60.
  • 1.9 million CT scans in 2024, and NFZ CT and MR volumes grew 74% and 87% between 2019 and 2024. Radiologists are short, so time saved has real value.
  • Teleradiology companies (Telediagnosis, Voxel, MKJ, Teleradius, Affidea and others) buy reports in bulk. Telediagnosis says it handles 65,000 studies a month. Facilities in Mazovia pay about 200–230 zł per CT report.

Where Radiopis can be the best

Already built

  • Written by a practising emergency radiologist: urgency tiers, pertinent negatives, conclusions without incidental findings, consultation line.
  • English → Polish as a main path, with a personal glossary.
  • 249 templates covering trauma, non-trauma, children and combined studies.
  • Safety net: removes contradicted template sentences, highlights every change, flags mismatched numbers and sides.
  • Works in a narrow window beside the PACS, with PowerMic and voice commands.
  • No report archive on the server; option to run the AI in the EU.

Gaps competitors leave open

  • Public prices in złoty (none of the three publishes one).
  • An uptime promise and support in Polish (askRadek offers none).
  • A clear, written medical-device position.
  • Ready data-protection paperwork for clinics (DPA template, DPIA).
  • Per-report pricing for teleradiology companies.
  • KSeF invoices and BLIK payments.

What the AI costs per report

The app uses Claude Opus 5.5. Anthropic charges per token (roughly per 3–4 characters): $4 per million input tokens, $20 per million output tokens, $0.20 per million tokens read from the cache, $5 per million written to the cache. The app's long instruction block is cached, so most of each request is cheap. Note that the AI's internal reasoning is billed as output.

Part of one AI callTokensCost
Instructions (21,600 characters, cached, shared by all users)5,000–7,000$0.001
Template and your style (cached per user; written again after 5 idle minutes)1,000–6,000$0.0005–0.03
Dictation and current report (not cached)800–2,500$0.003–0.01
Answer plus reasoning (output)500–2,500$0.01–0.05
One call$0.015–0.08

One report can need more than one call: the app pre-processes during pauses in dictation so the report is ready the moment you press "Przygotuj opis". Background calls are now capped at 4 per report (adjustable in the admin panel).

Ways to lower the cost if needed

  • Lower the background-call limit, or switch background processing off for the cheapest plan.
  • Use Claude Sonnet 5.5 (half the price) for background processing or the cheapest plan. This needs a quality check first.
  • Keep "fast" mode (low reasoning effort) as the default; it is already the default.
  • Set cost alerts per user (built in: the admin gets an e-mail when a user's AI cost passes 50% of their plan price).

Why these numbers

  • Start sits just above Medly (199 zł, a general scribe) and well below MagicSpeech (≈ 453 zł a month, speech only).
  • Pro at 399 zł is less than one or two CT reports' worth of a radiologist's time a month, and still below Heidi or Dragon in złoty terms.
  • Annual plans cost 10 months' price (2 months free). This improves cash flow and keeps customers.
  • Founding-member offer: 30% off for 12 months for the first 50 paying users (Start 174 zł, Pro 279 zł). This answers the free askRadek beta without cutting list prices. (Discount codes work in Stripe Checkout.)

How to win the Polish market

1. Prove it with colleagues

Free beta for 5–10 radiologists you trust (emergency and teleradiology). Measure real AI cost, time saved per report and errors. Collect two or three named testimonials.

2. Sell the emergency story

"Built by an emergency radiologist for night shifts": urgency-ordered findings, clean conclusions, English → Polish. A 60-second demo video of a polytrauma report.

3. Go where radiologists are

Polish Radiological Society (PLTR) congress and regional meetings, Facebook and LinkedIn groups for radiologists, resident networks (consider a discounted resident plan).

4. Teleradiology companies

Their radiologists are paid per report, so time saved is money. Offer a pilot: 1 month free for 5 readers, then per-report pricing and one monthly invoice.

5. Referrals

One free month for both sides when a colleague subscribes. Radiology is a small, connected community.

6. Trust as a feature

Publish the price, the privacy model (no report archive, PESEL removed, EU processing option), the medical-device position and an uptime promise. Competitors publish none of these.

What you, the administrator, can control (already built)

  • Each user: plan, monthly limit, access date, status, block, log out of all devices, reset link, notes, payments, devices, full history.
  • Money: monthly recurring revenue, payments this month, AI cost and margin per user and in total, cost per report, calls per report, CSV exports for the accountant.
  • Automatic: expiry, e-mail reminders 7 and 1 day before the end, grace days after a failed card payment, cost alerts, limits with 10% tolerance, device limits against account sharing, a cap on background AI calls per report.
  • Prices and plans: edit prices, limits and devices, link Stripe prices, see margins in the built-in calculator.

Legal and admin checklist before charging

Medical-device opinion (MDR)Software that writes conclusions may count as a medical device. Sweden treated one AI scribe as at least Class IIa; the UK says summarising alone is not enough. Get a written opinion from a regulatory consultant and fix the "intended purpose" wording accordingly.to do
Data protection (RODO)Health data is special-category data. You need a processing agreement (umowa powierzenia) for clinics and doctors, a DPIA, and an agreement with the AI provider: Anthropic's commercial terms and DPA (zero data retention is arranged through their sales team), or Claude on Amazon Bedrock in Frankfurt for EU processing. The Anthropic API itself offers only "global" or "US" processing.partly built

Code protection, honestly: nobody can see the app without a paid login, the AI instructions never leave the server, and the browser code is scrambled (obfuscated). A determined programmer with a paid account could still study the scrambled code; no web app can fully prevent that. The terms forbid copying, and a registered trademark plus the server-side AI instructions are your real protection.

Launch roadmap

Weeks 1–2
Choose and check the name and domain. Open a Stripe account and an EU server (or ask me to prepare step-by-step instructions for a hosting provider). Set up Resend for e-mail. Start the MDR and RODO conversations.
Weeks 3–6
Private beta with 5–10 colleagues, free. Watch real cost per report, errors and feedback in the admin panel. Improve templates and wording with them.
Month 2–3
Fix prices from real costs. Finish the legal documents and KSeF invoicing. Public launch with the founding-member offer, demo video and a talk or stand at a radiology meeting.
Month 3–6
First teleradiology pilot. Team accounts (one invoice, many users). Better speech recognition (Azure Speech or ElevenLabs, tested on real dictations). Italian version if there is demand.
