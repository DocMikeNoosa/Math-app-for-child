# EndoLetter: quick start

1. Save **EndoLetter.html** somewhere permanent, e.g. Documents. Don't run it from Downloads, since files there get cleaned up.
2. Open it in **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**. You can right-click it and choose Open with, then make a bookmark.
3. **Settings:** enter your practice name, name, credentials, address and phone, and upload a logo and signature (optional).
4. Click **Choose folder…** and pick or create a folder, e.g. `Documents/Endo Letters`.
5. **Case tab:** enter the patient and referring dentist, click the tooth on the chart, and record tests and treatment.
   The diagnosis is suggested automatically. You can override it.
6. **Save letter PDF.** The letter is filed at
   `Endo Letters/Letters/<Last, First (DOB …)>/<YYYY-MM-DD> - <Last, First> - Tooth NN <procedure>.pdf`
   with the chart note (.txt) saved next to it.

## Your data
- Every change saves inside the app and is backed up to `Endo Letters/App Data (do not edit)/`.
  That folder holds the latest copy, daily snapshots for 60 days, and the radiographs.
- After a computer restart, Chrome may show a **Reconnect** banner. That's one click to allow folder access again.
- If browser data is ever cleared, open the app and choose the same folder. Everything is restored.
- Nothing is sent over the internet. The app works offline.
